What's on the calendar

There's always something happening here

The calendar changes monthly, but the spirit doesn't. Here's the programming that runs through the year, and October's flyer exactly as Ren's posted it.

♫

Open Mic Night

Poems, songs, first drafts, brave ideas. Bring something to share or bring a friend and cheer. One of the friendliest rooms in South Jersey for trying new material.

📚

Story Hour with Libby & Alicia

Kids' reading time at the shop, snacks from the kids' menu, and parents who get to finish a coffee while it's still warm.

🐈

Crafting with Cats

Crafts, company, and cats. Exactly what it sounds like, and exactly why people keep coming back.

🧘

Roots, Roast & Reads: Outdoor Yoga with Tabbi

An evening yoga session outside the shop, paired with a drink from the bar afterward.

⚔️

Ren Faire Weekend

A Renaissance-themed weekend in a bookstore cafe. Costumes encouraged. Yes, really.

🎃

Seasonal festivals

We show up for Blackwood's community events like the Pumpkin Fest, and we run our own challenges, like the summer reading bingo that fills the shop with competing bookworms every June.
