Summer Reading Bingo

Ren's summer reading challenge, run the way the shop runs it.

Where the 2026 challenge stands

The 2026 challenge ran June 27 through August 31 and has wrapped. The rules stay up as a reference. Watch the calendar or ask at the counter about next summer's board.

During Ren's Summer Reading Bingo Challenge, read a book that fits each prompt. Write the title and author to complete the prompt in its square. One book may be used a maximum of twice per card!

Looking for a book to fill a specific prompt? The shop shares recommendations for each prompt through the summer. Complete as many bingos as possible! Prizes range from 1 completed straight line bingo, horizontal, vertical, or diagonal, to an entire bingo card blackout.

In 2026, prizes could be redeemed only when you finished the challenge, on or before August 31, and completed cards were handed to a staff member in store.
