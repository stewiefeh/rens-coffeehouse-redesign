Why people keep coming back

One building, two obsessions

Coffee worth the trip

Beans from a local roaster, hand-crafted espresso drinks, and a seasonal board that changes with the weather. Pastries and cookies from local bakers.

A real bookstore

New and used titles, local authors, a kids' section, and handmade goods from local artists, all in labeled sections you can actually browse.

Open mic nights, story hour, crafting with cats, outdoor yoga, themed weekends. There is almost always something on the calendar.

The atmosphere

Small room, big welcome

Sixteen seats inside, sidewalk tables out front, and staff who remember your order. Grab a coffee, pick a book off the shelf, play a board game, or find a quiet corner to study. The point of Ren's is that you're allowed to be exactly yourself here.

Plan your visit

This fall at the espresso bar

The Top 16

Sixteen specials for the season, shaken and topped by hand. Here's the first batch. The full lineup lives on the menu.

S'mores Remix

Dark chocolate shaken espresso with marshmallow cold foam and graham crackers, served in a marshmallow-sheared cup.

The Black(wood) Parade

Dark chocolate and wild raspberry latte, whipped cream on top, raspberry drizzle, bat sprinkles. A love letter to our town.

All the Fall Things

Pumpkin cold brew topped with pumpkin cold foam, a maple drizzle, and cinnamon.

Pumpkin Fest

Pumpkin spice shaken espresso finished with cinnamon.

The Smashing Apples

Caramel apple butter americano with a honey drizzle on the cup.

Pierce the Vanilla

Butterscotch and French vanilla americano. Quietly the regulars' pick.
