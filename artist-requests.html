Artist Requests

The shop carries handcrafted work from local artists and makers, from prints to pressed-flower bookmarks.

At Ren's Coffeehouse & Books, we love to support our local artists and makers! Offering handcrafted items made by those in our community is deeply meaningful to us.

Due to the volume of requests received each month, and the backlog we currently have under review, we are taking a pause on accepting new submissions at this time.

Thank you for your interest, enthusiasm, and your patience and understanding. Please check back here in the future for when our submission form is reactivated!

Questions in the meantime?

Say hi at the counter or email renscoffeenj@gmail.com.
