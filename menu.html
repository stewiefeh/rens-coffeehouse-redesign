Fall Top 16

The seasonals

Sixteen specials for fall, shaken and topped by hand. Six get the spotlight below; the full sixteen are listed just past them.

Pastries & cookies

A rotating assortment baked in and sourced locally, with gluten-free options when you ask. Kids' snacks too: string cheese, cake pops, GoGo SqueeZ, Aunt Tinnie's cookies.

Coffee-to-go

Feeding a crowd? A $22 box of coffee covers the meeting, the book club, or the pumpkin carving night.

S'mores Remix

Dark chocolate shaken espresso with marshmallow cold foam and graham crackers, served in a marshmallow-sheared cup.

The Black(wood) Parade

Dark chocolate and wild raspberry latte, whipped cream on top, raspberry drizzle, bat sprinkles. A love letter to our town.

All the Fall Things

Pumpkin cold brew topped with pumpkin cold foam, a maple drizzle, and cinnamon.

Pumpkin Fest

Pumpkin spice shaken espresso finished with cinnamon.

The Smashing Apples

Caramel apple butter Aerocano with a honey drizzle on the cup.

Pierce the Vanilla

Butterscotch and French vanilla Aerocano. Quietly the regulars' pick.

The full fall 16

Every seasonal, named

Sunday Scaries

Blueberry cinnamon bun latte topped with cinnamon bun cold foam and cinnamon sugar.

Cookie Monster

Biscoff and spiced brown sugar latte topped with cinnamon bun cold foam and graham crackers.

The Salty Pumpkin

Pumpkin and vanilla salt latte topped with whip and black lava salt.

It's Not a Phase

Dark and white chocolate latte topped with whip and emo sprinkles.

Apple Jax

Caramel apple butter latte topped with whip, cinnamon, and Apple Jacks cereal.

Berry Wyld

Raspberry and butterscotch latte topped with whip and cinnamon sugar.

Mrs. Brightside

Caramel latte topped with blueberry cold foam and a caramel drizzle.

Chai Chemical Romance

French vanilla dirty chai topped with pumpkin cold foam.

Sorry I'm Late, I Didn't Want to Be Here

Cookie butter latte topped with pumpkin cold foam and biscoff crumbles.

Salted Caramel Pretzel

Salted caramel latte topped with whip, a caramel drizzle, and pretzel pieces.

The board marks the first five iced only. The rest aren't marked either way, so ask at the counter.
