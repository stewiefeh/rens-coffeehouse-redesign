About

A coffeehouse and a bookstore under one roof

Ren's opened on June 27, 2024 at 26 N Black Horse Pike in Blackwood. Here's the shop in its own words.

Ren's is a coffeehouse and bookstore! Our café uses coffee beans sourced from a local roaster to ensure your cup is always fresh and made with the best quality. We offer hand-crafted drinks, an assortment of pastries, and cookies too!

Check out our bookstore – it is filled with a mix of new and used books, fun bookish gifts, books by local authors and children's authors, as well as many items made by local artists!

We aim to create an atmosphere that feels safe and comfortable; a place to grab a coffee, read a book, play a board game, bring your kids to reading time, express your creativity at our open mic, or just have a quiet place to study.
