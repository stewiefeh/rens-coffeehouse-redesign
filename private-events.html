Private events

Book the whole room

A cozy, inviting space for the nights that matter: book clubs, birthdays, showers, writing groups, team nights.

How it works

👥

Up to 30 guests

The room holds private reservations of up to 30 people, which is exactly the size where everyone still hears each other.

💳

A deposit holds your date

A 50% reservation deposit books the space. If plans shift, that value moves to a future reservation, so a rain check never costs you the money.

☕

Coffee included, obviously

Drink service from the bar, a coffee-to-go box option for the table, and pastries to go with it.

Ask about a date

Email gets the fastest answer. Rates and policies are below, straight from the house rules.

The house rules, in plain terms

🕑

Your window covers everything

Setup, the event, and cleanup all live inside the reservation block. Most events need 30 minutes on each end, and two-hour blocks are the sweet spot. Need longer? Say so when you book.

🍽️

Bring your own, within reason

Outside food and drinks are welcome as long as your group tidies up before leaving. Beer and wine can come too; hard liquor can't. Please drink responsibly.

📚

The bookstore stays open

The shop is yours to browse all event, and your guests take 10% off any merchandise they buy that day.

💰

How the money works

The 50% deposit is non-refundable, but if plans change it rolls into a future reservation or store credit, no penalty. The balance is due when the event starts. A refundable damage deposit may apply, pricing can shift by season, and NJ's 6.625% sales tax applies to it all.

🌙

After-hours works

Private reservations can run outside store hours at their own rates. Want the whole place during open hours? That gets priced case by case.
